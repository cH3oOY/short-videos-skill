# Romance

## 默认策略

优先使用 slow push-in、gentle arc、steadicam follow 和 rack focus。速度慢，运动终点应落在眼神、手部或两人关系上。

## Prompt

```text
Medium two-shot at eye level, two people sit across from each other in a quiet cafe during golden hour. One person reaches across the table and gently touches the other's hand. The camera performs a slow 45-degree arc around them over 5 seconds, moving from a balanced two-shot to a more intimate over-the-shoulder composition. Keep both faces natural and recognizable, soft window light, shallow depth of field, warm muted colors, no cuts, gentle romantic realism, 16:9.
```
