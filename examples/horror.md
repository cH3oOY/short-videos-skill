# Horror

## 默认策略

优先使用慢速 push-in、静止镜头中的主体动作、前景遮挡揭示和轻微 handheld。恐怖感来自等待、空间和声音暗示，不要用无意义的高速旋转。

## Prompt

```text
Locked-off wide shot of an empty child's bedroom at 2 a.m. A door in the background opens by itself one inch. Hold the frame completely still for the first 2 seconds, then perform a very slow dolly-in toward the dark gap over the next 3 seconds. The room remains unchanged, no visible person, no cut, no jump scare, low-key moonlight, deep shadows, subtle film grain, restrained psychological horror, 16:9.
```
