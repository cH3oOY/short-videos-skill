# Product

## 默认策略

优先使用 macro slide、slow orbit、controlled dolly 和 pedestal。明确产品标签、形状、材质和反光必须保持一致。

## Prompt

```text
Macro close-up of a white ceramic cup on a dark wooden table, a thin stream of coffee pouring into it. The camera performs a slow lateral truck from left to right over 4 seconds, revealing the cup handle and rising steam while keeping the logo perfectly readable. Stable product framing, controlled specular highlights, soft studio key light, shallow depth of field, premium commercial style, no text changes, no extra objects, no cuts, 9:16 vertical.
```
