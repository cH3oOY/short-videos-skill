# Action

## 默认策略

使用 tracking、whip pan、handheld 或 FPV，但每个片段只保留一个主方向。动作方向要明确，避免主体在镜头中随机漂移。

## Prompt

```text
Low-angle medium shot of a motorcycle rider accelerating through a narrow city alley. The camera performs a fast forward tracking shot from behind, matching the rider's direction for 4 seconds, then whips right as the rider turns into a side street. Strong but readable motion blur, controlled handheld vibration, wet pavement reflections, no extra vehicles, continuous action, cinematic night lighting, 16:9.
```
