# Chinese Fantasy

## 默认策略

优先使用 crane、aerial rise、slow orbit、低机位 push-in 和前景云雾 reveal。先建立山河、宫殿或战场的空间关系，再让主体进入动作。

## Prompt

```text
Wide low-angle shot of a young sword cultivator standing on a cliff above a vast sea of clouds at dawn. The camera slowly cranes upward 6 meters and arcs 60 degrees around the cultivator over 6 seconds, revealing a floating celestial palace in the background. The cultivator remains centered, robe and hair move gently in the wind, facial identity and costume stay consistent. Golden sunrise rim light, blue-white atmospheric haze, elegant Chinese fantasy cinema, smooth motion, no cuts, 2.39:1.
```
