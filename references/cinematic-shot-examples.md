# Cinematic Shot Examples

## 示例 1：恐惧逼近

```text
Medium close-up, eye-level, a woman stands alone in a dim apartment hallway at night. She hears a faint footstep behind her and slowly raises her eyes. The camera performs a creeping dolly-in from medium close-up to tight close-up over 5 seconds, moving forward 1 meter at a nearly constant speed and easing to a stop when her eyes widen. Keep her face, hair, clothing, hallway geometry, and light direction unchanged. Her eyes remain sharp. Single continuous shot, no cuts, no zoom, no orbit. Cold window light, deep shadows, restrained psychological horror, 16:9.
```

## 示例 2：人物登场

```text
Wide shot, low angle, a lone warrior stands on a ridge above a sea of clouds at sunrise. The camera cranes upward 8 meters while slowly tilting down, transitioning from the warrior's boots to the vast mountain range behind him over 6 seconds. The warrior remains centered and still, cloak moving gently in the wind. Smooth ease-in and ease-out, no cuts, epic scale, warm rim light, atmospheric haze, cinematic Chinese fantasy, 2.39:1.
```

## 示例 3：产品展示

```text
Macro close-up of a matte black perfume bottle on wet stone, droplets glistening under a narrow softbox. The camera performs a slow 120-degree orbit around the bottle over 5 seconds at a constant radius, keeping the label perfectly readable and the bottle centered. Controlled reflections, shallow depth of field, subtle parallax, premium commercial lighting, no extra objects, no text changes, no cuts, 9:16 vertical.
```

## 示例 4：Dolly Zoom

```text
Medium close-up, a man stands at the edge of a high stairwell and suddenly looks downward. His body remains approximately the same size in frame while the camera dollies forward 1.5 meters and the lens zooms out over 3 seconds. The stairwell behind him stretches dramatically and the perspective becomes disorienting. Keep his eyes sharp and his position centered. Controlled motion blur, single continuous shot, no cut, psychological vertigo, muted green and amber color grade.
```

## 示例 5：横向跟拍

```text
Medium profile shot, a young woman runs through a rain-soaked market street at dusk. The camera tracks smoothly from her left side at a constant 3-meter distance, matching her speed for 5 seconds. Market stalls create strong foreground parallax while she remains frame-right and sharp. Rain direction and neon reflections stay consistent. Gimbal-stabilized movement with natural motion blur, no cuts, energetic but readable, 16:9.
```
